# Changelog

## v115
- Workshop minigames are 35% slower at every tool level. "Tap to start" and a 3, 2, 1 countdown (the Grip Etcher starts when you touch its tip; the Kiln counts down on its own).

## v114
- Every workshop session is a minigame: Buff rhythm (Polisher), Trace the groove (Grip Etcher), Keep it level (Balancing Rig), Heat it right (Hardening Kiln), Seal the cracks (Repair Bench). Perfect gives 150%, Good 100%, OK 60%, Miss 25%. Skip gives 40–80% by tool level.
- Garage split into Marbles and Tools tabs; tool upgrades raise the gain and make the minigame easier.

## v113
- Races play 25% faster (about 2:30 instead of 3:05), lifts 40% faster, a lower and closer chase camera, a quicker start, fast-forward once your marbles are home, and commentary in quiet stretches.
- Fixed: a shattered marble could stop the race from ending. Fixed: fan loss after a bad weekend never applied.

## v112
- Rival marbles' traits show in the race too (the 4 nearest, quieter, grey tags).

## v111
- Music: three menu tracks and two race tracks, with a music button (On, Menus only, Off).

## v110
- Last-lap charge: when your marble gains 3+ places in 5 seconds on the last lap, the camera cuts to it with a zoom and a short slow motion.

## v108–v109
- Race sound effects tried and removed.

## v107
- Trait balance pass. Sprinter is now Lift launcher (burst off every lift), Rocket start is Pole hunter (3 grid places up), Bouncy is Rebound (wall hits give some speed back).

## v106
- Gacha capsule no longer passes through the cabinet; podium trophies lit properly.

## v105
- Stats and traits rework: Toughness = cracks and wear, Stability = wobbles, Weight shown as Light / Medium / Heavy. Crack risk on every marble and a warning before racing one that can shatter. Tank is now Unbreakable, Heavy is now Steamroller.

## v104
